Calculation method & source limitations

Employee CNAS is 9% of gross. IRG uses progressive rates of 0%, 23%, 27%, 30%, 33% and 35%, followed by a 40% tax reduction bounded between 1,000 and 1,500 DA. Taxable salaries up to 30,000 DA are exempt.

The PDF’s smoothing formulas ambiguously reference tax before relief. This calculator applies them after the standard reduction for taxable salaries from 30,000 to 35,000 DA (or 42,500 DA for eligible workers). Confirm this interpretation and current exemption limits with the DGI before payroll use.

The PDF also asks readers to verify the 25.5% employer contribution rate. Calculations retain decimal precision and display two decimal places; actual payroll rounding can differ. This is an estimate, not a certified payslip.
